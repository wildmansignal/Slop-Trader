How this works

Three modes:

  • PAPER — simulated fills against live Gate.io prices. Free to run, no risk.
  • SIGNAL ONLY — alerts only, no trades. Use for manual execution.
  • LIVE (webhook) — POSTs signals as JSON to your local server. Your Python bot (e.g. gate_pip_bot.py) holds the exchange keys and places real orders. Browser never touches keys.

Strategies running concurrently:

  • Trend Follow — EMA9 cross EMA21 with EMA50 as direction filter, ATR stop.
  • QFL (Quick Fingers Luc) — finds local "base" levels (lows that held), buys when price snaps below the base by ≥1.5% and prints a reversal candle.
  • 5m Panic Dip — drops >2.5% in 3 bars + volume >2× average, waits for green reversal candle.
  • Mean Revert — RSI <25 / >75 with band tag.
  • Millipede (PipEasy) — bidirectional, multi-timeframe trend stacker. The strategy that exploits structural asymmetry: take both longs and shorts on signal, breakeven the stop the moment a side moves in your favor. Losers cost ~$0, winners run with a 1R-step trailing stop. Long branch fires in weekly uptrends on a daily pullback to 21EMA + green reversal; short branch fires in weekly downtrends on a bounce to 21EMA + red reversal. Each new soldier only opens when the prior same-side soldier's stop is at breakeven AND in profit. Stops only ever move in the trade's favor. Cap: 5 soldiers per side per symbol (configurable). Live trading requires a hedge-mode futures exchange — Binance Futures, Bybit, Gate.io Futures (different product than your spot bot), Hyperliquid, or dYdX. Spot exchanges net positions and will collapse the hedge. Free Coins Vault: long profits → free coin pile; short profits → USDT pile (don't buy back what you just rode down).
  • AI Router — reads Fear & Greed, BTC trend, ATR-volatility, then enables/disables strategies per regime.

Free Coins Rule: on every winning close, X% of the dollar profit is converted into the coin you just traded and stashed in the vault. The vault never trades — it only grows. If SOL goes to the moon, your free SOL pile rides it.

⚠ Before going LIVE: rotate your hardcoded Gate.io keys in gate_pip_bot.py. Move them to env vars. Audit every signal in PAPER mode for at least 2 weeks of normal market conditions before risking real money.
✓ This UI never sees your exchange keys. Live execution goes through your own webhook to your own bot. The Anthropic key (if used) stays in browser localStorage and is only sent directly to api.anthropic.com.

Webhook payload format

In LIVE mode, signals are POSTed to your webhook URL as:

{
  "ts": 1730000000000,
  "symbol": "SOLUSDT",
  "side": "buy" | "sell",
  "strategy": "qfl" | "trend" | "panic" | "meanrev",
  "price": 165.42,
  "size_usd": 500,
  "stop_loss": 162.10,
  "take_profit": 170.50,
  "reason": "QFL base break + reversal at 162.30",
  "free_coin_lock_pct": 100
}

Minimal Python receiver (Flask) — pair this with your existing gate_pip_bot.py order placer:

from flask import Flask, request
app = Flask(__name__)
@app.post("/signal")
def signal():
    s = request.json
    if s["side"] == "buy":
        place_buy(s["symbol"], s["size_usd"], s["stop_loss"], s["take_profit"])
    return "ok"
app.run(port=5055)
